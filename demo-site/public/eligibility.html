Academic Requisites

Applicant must possess a recognized post-baccalaureate credential in disciplines cataloged under Schedule IV, obtaining no less than 65% aggregate normalized percentile or equivalent CGPA in penultimate assessments.

Financial & Domiciliary Requisite

Family cumulative gross annual accrual must remain within statutory ceilings as promulgated under Notification 88/2025.

* Note: Gross annual family income cap is set at ₹8,00,000 per annum verified by Form 16 or Tahsildar Certificate.
